### 1. highways-act JSON-LD: @graph instead of flat Article

**File:** `how-to/highways-act-section-154-overhanging-vegetation-notice.html`

The JSON-LD on this page uses `@graph` with three schema types: `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage`. Per TEMPLATES/how-to.md (§2), individual how-to articles must use a flat `Article` object only. `@graph` is reserved for `how-to/index.html`. The BreadcrumbList and FAQPage wrappers need to be removed and the JSON-LD reduced to a plain flat Article. This requires a judgment call on whether to retain the FAQ content in a different form, so it is flagged for manual review.
