### Bridge-specific angles developed

1. The Management of Hedgerows (England) Regulations 2024 (SI 2024/680): applies to field boundary hedges on/adjacent to agricultural land, prohibits cutting 1 March to 31 August, in force 23 May 2024. The article focuses on the contractor compliance angle, complementing the PTES article's coverage of the ecological case for the same regulations.
2. The distinction between agricultural hedgerows (covered by both the 2024 Regulations and WCA 1981 s.1) and garden hedges (WCA 1981 s.1 only), with cross-link to the nesting law guide.
3. North Downs chalk slopes vs Nailbourne valley floor: different species mix and growth rates, hawthorn/blackthorn/elder/dog rose/field maple/wayfaring tree on chalk; elder and dog rose faster on wetter valley floor.
4. Badger sett checks before mechanical hedge cutting on rural field boundary hedges (Protection of Badgers Act 1992): a practical step not covered by the UKCEH film and specific to working on established rural hedgerows.
5. Bifrons Park Conservation Area: section 211 TCPA 1990 notices for stems above 75mm at 1.5m height.
