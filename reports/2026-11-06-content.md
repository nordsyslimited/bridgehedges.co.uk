## Bridge/Nailbourne-valley specificity

- North Downs chalk alkalinity (high pH) incompatibility with calcifuge rhododendrons
- Distinction between chalk slopes above Bridge and the clay-loam valley floor
- Yakushimanum and PJM group as marginally more tolerant cultivars
- Nesting law: flowering in May/June puts the pruning window squarely within 1 March to 31 August (Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, s.1)
- Section 211 Town and Country Planning Act 1990 for large specimens in Bifrons Park CA
- Alternatives on chalk: Viburnum tinus, Escallonia 'Iveyi', Choisya ternata, mixed native hedge
