## Needs review

### NR-1: Coastal content carry-over in services/tree pages (6 pages)

All five services/tree pages and the tree services index contain scaffold copy from a coastal ring-site sibling. Bridge is an inland Nailbourne-valley/North Downs village, not a coastal town. The carry-over is present in:

**Footer text** (all 5 tree pages + index, 6 files):
> "Local, coast-aware hedge care and tree work for Bridge, the clifftop bays and St Peter's."

"The clifftop bays" and "St Peter's" as a coastal area are not Bridge. The correct footer copy on all other pages reads: "Local hedge care for Bridge and the Nailbourne valley villages."

**Body text — specific instances:**

- `services/tree/crown-thinning.html` (lines 33, 47, 51): "clifftop or seafront plot", "Bridge seafront and clifftop plots", "Coastal Monterey pine". JSON-LD description also has "clifftop plots".
- `services/tree/crown-raising.html` (lines 27, 40, 45, 72): "sightline down towards the seafront", "clifftop or exposed seafront plot", "raises cleanly on the seafront plots".
- `services/tree/crown-reduction.html` (line 8 meta description): "dropping clifftop wind-loading". Body: "seafront plots", "coastal Monterey pine".
- `services/tree/deadwooding.html` (line 42): "Coastal wind exposure moves the cycle a touch tighter, dead branches on a clifftop tree".
- `services/tree/index.html` (lines 42, 59, 74): "Clifftop wind-load", "sightline down towards the seafront", "The usual answer when a mature front-garden tree on Gladstone Road or Rectory Road", "coastal hedge specialist". "Gladstone Road" is a suspect street name, not a known Bridge, Kent address.
- `services/tree/pruning.html` (lines 37–41): "Bridge seafront plots and along Stone Road", "the seafront belt", "Bridge and St Peter's plots and in the smaller cottage gardens off Albion Street". "Stone Road" and "Albion Street" are not known Bridge, Kent addresses.

Action needed: rewrite the 6 affected footer paragraphs and audit/rewrite the body-text instances in each of the 6 pages. The correct footer variant is already in use on all non-tree pages.
