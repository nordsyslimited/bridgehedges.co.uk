## Bridge/Nailbourne-valley specificity

1. **Box substitute context** - box blight (Cylindrocladium buxicola) and box tree moth (Cydalima perspectalis) have made Buxus sempervirens hedging increasingly difficult in Kent; E. japonicus 'Microphyllus' is the closest available compact evergreen substitute on chalk ground.
2. **Chalk soil compatibility** - species is alkaline-tolerant, H5 rated, reliable on North Downs chalk slopes and valley-side ground.
3. **Nesting-season timing conflict** - June clip falls squarely within WCA 1981 s.1 window (1 March to 31 August); dense compact evergreen growth used by wren, robin and dunnock. Visual inspection step and alternatives described.
4. **Powdery mildew on free-draining chalk** - noted as a dry-year risk on chalk soils around Bridge; response and mulching advice given.
5. **Frost hardiness** - H5, fully hardy throughout the Nailbourne valley including frost-pocket valley-floor gardens.
6. **Bifrons Park Conservation Area** - s.211 TCPA 1990 check note; compact forms rarely reach 75mm threshold in routine hedging use.
7. Named villages: Bridge, Patrixbourne, Bekesbourne, Bishopsbourne, Barham, Kingston, Lower Hardres.
