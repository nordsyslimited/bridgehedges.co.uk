## Bridge/Nailbourne valley specificity

Five distinct local points added in the "What it doesn't cover" section:

1. **French vs English lavender for North Downs hedging:** English lavender (Hidcote, Munstead, Vera) is hardier and more reliable for a long-term hedge on the chalk slopes; French lavender (Lavandula stoechas) is showier but less frost-tolerant.
2. **Chalk soil advantage:** North Downs chalk is excellent lavender ground (free-draining, alkaline), matching what lavender needs. Lean chalk also suppresses the floppy growth that rich soil produces.
3. **Nailbourne valley floor:** French lavender unsuitable for frost pockets and heavier valley soils; even English lavender needs sharp drainage on valley-floor sites. Rosemary on a raised bed referenced as an alternative.
4. **Nesting season timing:** Late-spring/summer trim falls within the 1 March to 31 August window of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, s.1. Nest-check steps covered; September trim as the safest option for active hedges.
5. **Bifrons Park CA:** Lavender stems very unlikely to reach the 75mm threshold under s.211 Town and Country Planning Act 1990, so routine trimming within the CA does not require prior notification. Old specimen caveat noted.
