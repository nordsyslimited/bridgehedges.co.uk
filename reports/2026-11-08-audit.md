**Required fix:** Replace the `@graph` JSON-LD block with a flat `Article` object. The `FAQPage` and `BreadcrumbList` data can be dropped entirely.

The filename does not contain "video" so this is not automatically a violation, but all how-to articles are supposed to be video-companion-led per the site's structural angle. Flag for assessment: either add a suitable video or accept this as an edge case for a legal-notice topic.
