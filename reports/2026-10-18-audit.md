### 4. `highways-act-section-154-overhanging-vegetation-notice.html` — three template violations (NEW, shipped 2026-10-17)

This article has good content and accurate local detail, but it was shipped with three deviations from the template contract:

**a. No video companion (structural-angle drift)**
The article has no YouTube video anchor, no "Watch first" callout and no yt-play button. This site's assigned structural angle is video-companion-led: every how-to article is built around one specific, real, publicly-available UK hedge-care or garden-law YouTube video. There are relevant UK videos on highway/council notices and vegetation law (e.g. Highways Act explainers). A companion video should be identified and added, or — if none exists — the article should carry a note explaining the exception.

**b. `<title>` missing site suffix**
Current: `<title>Received a Section 154 Notice in Bridge? What to Do About an Overhanging Hedge or Tree</title>`
Required (P1): `<title>… | Bridge Hedges &amp; Tree Services</title>`

**c. JSON-LD uses `@graph` with BreadcrumbList and FAQPage**
The template contract (TEMPLATES/how-to.md §2) requires individual how-to articles to use a flat `Article` object with no `@graph` wrapper, no `BreadcrumbList` and no `FAQPage`. The only page on the site that should use `@graph` is `how-to/index.html`. This article's JSON-LD block uses `@graph` containing Article + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage. The BreadcrumbList and FAQPage wrappers should be removed, leaving a plain flat Article block. The FAQ content itself can stay in the page HTML.
