## Bridge/Nailbourne specificity

The article's "What it doesn't cover" section addresses:

- Nesting-season conflict: hard pruning in late spring falls within WCA 1981 s.1 window; survey required before any renovation cut between March and August
- Valley-floor frost-pocket delay: wait until mid-May on the Nailbourne valley floor (Bishopsbourne/Barham sections); chalk slopes above Bridge are safe from late April
- Chalk-soil recovery: free-draining chalk means watering and mulching matter in the first growing season after a hard cut
- Winter hardiness: griselinia took damage in the 2010/11 UK winters; exposed valley-floor positions are marginal; alternatives discussed (hornbeam for valley floor, yew for chalk slopes)
- Bifrons Park Conservation Area: s.211 TCPA 1990 notice if stems reach 75mm diameter at 1.5m
