## Needs review

### 1. NEW — JSON-LD publisher name HTML-encoded in 2 new articles

`privet-hedge-trimming-video-bridge.html` (line 25) and `photinia-red-robin-hedge-pruning-video-bridge.html` (line 25) both have the publisher name encoded as HTML entity inside the JSON-LD `<script>` block:

```json
"publisher":{"@type":"Organization","name":"Bridge Hedges &amp; Tree Services"}
```

`<script>` tag content is not HTML-parsed — character references are not decoded. Google's structured data parser will therefore see the publisher name as the literal string `Bridge Hedges &amp; Tree Services` rather than `Bridge Hedges & Tree Services`. All other 13 articles use the correct bare ampersand.

**Fix**: change `&amp;` to `&` in the `publisher.name` field of both affected articles' JSON-LD blocks.
