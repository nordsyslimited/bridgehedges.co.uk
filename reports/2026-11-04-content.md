## Local specificity added

- Hardiness comparison: O. scilloniensis (Isles of Scilly origin, tender) versus O. x haastii (AGM, more frost-tolerant) for Bridge's inland chalk and valley-floor conditions
- Chalk slope suitability: free-draining chalk suits olearia's drainage requirements; south- and west-facing positions above Bridge toward Bekesbourne and Lower Hardres
- Valley floor caution: frost-pocket sites toward Bishopsbourne and Barham, moisture-retaining soil; O. x haastii still viable, O. scilloniensis not recommended
- Nesting-season law: post-flowering cut falls June-July, within the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 s.1 window (1 March-31 August); practical nest-check guidance given
- Conservation area note: 75mm stem threshold under Town and Country Planning Act 1990 s.211, Bifrons Park CA
