## Auto-fixed

### 1. JSON-LD publisher name HTML-encoded in two articles

`how-to/privet-hedge-trimming-video-bridge.html` and `how-to/photinia-red-robin-hedge-pruning-video-bridge.html` had `"name":"Bridge Hedges &amp; Tree Services"` in their JSON-LD `publisher` block. JSON-LD is JSON, not HTML; `&amp;` is a literal string in JSON and would appear as `Bridge Hedges &amp; Tree Services` in any structured data consumer. Changed to `"name":"Bridge Hedges & Tree Services"` in both files.
