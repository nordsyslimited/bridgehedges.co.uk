## Local specificity included

- North Downs chalk as the correct ground type for copper/common beech
- Nailbourne valley floor waterlogging incompatibility; hornbeam redirect
- Formative cut at planting (cut back by a third)
- Marcescent leaf retention, and its dependence on regular trimming
- Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 s.1 nesting-season window (1 March to 31 August)
- Bifrons Park Conservation Area s.211 TCPA 1990, 75mm threshold
- Late August to early September trimming timing for established hedge
