## Needs review

All 6 items are carry-overs from the 2026-11-19 audit. No new issues.

### 1. `how-to/highways-act-section-154-overhanging-vegetation-notice.html` — wrong JSON-LD schema + no video embed

**JSON-LD**: uses `@graph` with `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage` instead of a flat `Article` object. Per the TEMPLATES/how-to.md contract and AGENTS.md, individual how-to articles must use a flat `Article` with no `@graph` wrapper. Only `how-to/index.html` uses `@graph`.

**No video embed**: the Watch-first callout and yt-play button are absent. The article is a legal/procedural guide (Highways Act s.154 notices) with no video spine, which is a structural-angle drift from the video-companion house style. This is the only how-to article without a video.

**Action required**: decide whether to retrofit a suitable video and convert the JSON-LD to flat `Article`, or accept this article as a deliberate exception to house style and document that exception.

### 2. `how-to/lonicera-nitida-hedge-renovation-video-bridge.html` — Watch-first callout empty (yt-play button missing)

The callout heading "Watch first: Hedge RENOVATION, Trimming an old Hedge that Needs REVITALISING, RJL Gardens" is present but no `<button class="yt-play">` button has been placed inside it. The video ID is `gyhRxRdFERo` (confirmed in the Sources line at the bottom of the page). The click-to-play button markup needs adding inside the callout div.

### 3. `how-to/planting-bare-root-hedge-north-downs-chalk-bridge.html` — Watch-first callout empty (yt-play button missing)

Same issue: callout heading "Watch first: How to plant bare root hedges – Grow at Home, Royal Horticultural Society" is present but no yt-play button. Video ID `qDJc9rqCAY4` confirmed in Sources line.

### 4. `services/tree/pruning.html:34` — coastal content

Lines 34–35 reference "Bridge seafront plots", "Stone Road" and "salt-wind" in the holm oak and sycamore entries. Bridge is an inland Nailbourne-valley village on the North Downs, not a coastal town. This content appears to have survived from the Broadstairs-derived scaffold. Needs rewriting with accurate inland Bridge context.

### 5. `how-to/how-to-cut-trim-hedges-technique-video-bridge.html` and `how-to/when-to-cut-hedges-nesting-law-video-bridge.html` — shared YouTube ID

Both articles embed the same video (`_Dc3B2I7bu4`, Garden Ninja's hedge-cutting guide). Two distinct articles sharing one video ID is a structural problem: at a minimum one should be updated to use a genuinely different source video for the topic it covers.

### 6. YouTube video liveness

Video liveness (curl -sI checks against youtube.com) is unverifiable from the cloud container environment. No dead videos can be confirmed or cleared in this environment. Manual spot-checks recommended periodically from a non-restricted network.
