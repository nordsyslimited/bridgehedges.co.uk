## Bridge/Nailbourne-valley specificity

- Nesting-season law (WCA 1981 s.1, 1 March to 31 August): ivy is prime habitat for wrens, robins, blackbirds, song thrushes; timing constraint covered in detail.
- Bat roost risk (Schedule 5, WCA 1981; Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017): pipistrelle bats roost in established ivy on walls; stopping work if droppings found, Natural England licence requirement covered.
- October to February safe window established from both legal constraints.
- Old lime-mortar and flint-and-chalk walls in Bridge, Patrixbourne, Bekesbourne, Bishopsbourne: mortar condition assessment before stripping, frost exposure risk after removal, Historic England guidance referenced.
- Ivy's genuine ecological value discussed: autumn nectar for ivy bees, winter berries for fieldfares and redwings, year-round shelter -- managed reduction suggested as an alternative to full clearance where appropriate.
- Bifrons Park Conservation Area: noted that ivy is not a "tree" for s.211 TCPA 1990 purposes, so CA tree notification does not apply; listed building/wall consent implications flagged for listed structures; cross-link to CA guide included.
- Canterbury City Council as the relevant CA authority named.
