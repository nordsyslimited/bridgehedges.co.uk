## Needs review

### 1. Missing analytics.js on 2 service pages (NEW)

`services/hedge-planting.html` and `services/hedge-removal.html` have zero instances of `analytics.js`. Every non-redirect page requires `<script src="/assets/js/analytics.js?v=20261008" defer></script>` as the last element before `</body>`. These two are confirmed public, non-redirect content pages.

### 2. Coastal footer text in services/tree pages (carry-over)

Six pages retain the footer text "Local, coast-aware hedge care and tree work for Bridge, the clifftop bays and St Peter's." — a Broadstairs-derived string from the original site scaffold. Bridge is an inland Nailbourne-valley village, not coastal. The correct footer text is "Local hedge care for Bridge and the Nailbourne valley villages." (as used on all other pages).

Affected: `services/tree/index.html`, `services/tree/pruning.html`, `services/tree/crown-thinning.html`, `services/tree/crown-raising.html`, `services/tree/crown-reduction.html`, `services/tree/deadwooding.html`.

### 3. highways-act article: wrong JSON-LD schema + no video companion (carry-over)

`how-to/highways-act-section-154-overhanging-vegetation-notice.html` uses `@graph` with `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage` schemas. The template contract requires a flat `Article` object only for individual how-to articles; `@graph` is reserved for `how-to/index.html`. The article also has no click-to-play button or video embed of any kind, making it structurally different from the site's video-companion-led house style (it is a legal FAQ guide).

### 4. lonicera-nitida: empty "Watch first" callout, no yt-play button (carry-over)

`how-to/lonicera-nitida-hedge-renovation-video-bridge.html` has a `<div class="callout"><h4>Watch first: ...</h4></div>` with no button inside it. The video ID `gyhRxRdFERo` (RJL Gardens, Hedge RENOVATION) appears in the sources line but is not embedded. Requires the standard `<button type="button" class="yt-play" data-yt-id="gyhRxRdFERo" ...>` markup inside the callout.

### 5. planting-bare-root: empty "Watch first" callout, no yt-play button (carry-over)
