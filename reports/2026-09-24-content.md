## Bridge/Nailbourne-valley specificity notes

- Chalk slopes above Bridge toward Barham, Kingston and Lower Hardres: free-draining, alkaline, sometimes thin over rock
- Nailbourne valley floor (Bridge to Patrixbourne, Bekesbourne): heavier, wetter in winter when the Nailbourne flows, frost-pocket risk
- Native chalk species named as verified appropriate for North Downs: hawthorn (Crataegus monogyna), blackthorn (Prunus spinosa), field maple (Acer campestre), wayfaring tree (Viburnum lantana), hazel with caveats
- Waterlogging risk on valley floor: advice to break up compacted layer below planting trench
- Nesting law reference (s.1 WCA 1981) with contextual link to existing article
- Conservation area (Bifrons Park CA) reference for removal-before-replanting scenarios
- Wisley greensand vs North Downs chalk noted as a meaningful soil-type difference
