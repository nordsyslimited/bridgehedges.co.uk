## Bridge/Nailbourne-valley specificity

- Hardy fuchsia (Fuchsia magellanica) versus half-hardy cultivars: the distinction that determines whether a plant survives the winter at all
- Named cultivars for the valley floor: 'Riccartonii', 'Mrs Popple', 'Tom Thumb', straight species; more tender 'Hawkshead' and 'Versicolor' flagged as less reliable on exposed valley positions
- Frost-pocket timing: cut late April to early May on valley floor vs late March/early April on chalk slopes above Patrixbourne and Bekesbourne
- Wait until end of May before writing off a plant that shows no growth: roots can survive die-back to below soil level and push in June
- Nesting season: Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 s.1 (1 March to 31 August) falls across the entire spring pruning window; nest check procedure described
- Bifrons Park Conservation Area: fuchsia rarely reaches s.211 stem-diameter threshold, brief note included for old estate stock
- Cross-reference to nesting-law guide and conservation-area guide
