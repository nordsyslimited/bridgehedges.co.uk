## Topic-gap check

A search across all 40 existing how-to articles confirmed no article on jasmine (Jasminum officinale or J. nudiflorum) as a wall, fence or boundary plant. The covered species list was confirmed against the full how-to slug inventory before selecting this topic.

Jasmine is a relevant topic for Bridge because:
- Both J. officinale and J. nudiflorum are common wall and fence plants in Bridge and the Nailbourne villages
- The free-draining chalk of the North Downs suits both species well, and the distinction between summer and winter jasmine pruning timing is genuinely non-obvious
- The summer jasmine pruning window (August-September) falls at the boundary of the legal nesting season (WCA 1981 s.1), making timing advice locally important
- Winter jasmine pruning (March-April) lands in the nesting season, requiring care and a check-before-cut approach
- J. officinale is susceptible to dieback in the frost-pocket conditions of the Nailbourne valley floor, a local factor not covered in any general pruning guide
