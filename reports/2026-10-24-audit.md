### 2. planting-bare-root-hedge-north-downs-chalk-bridge.html — missing yt-play button

Same issue. "Watch first" callout has only the `<h4>` title. Video ID `qDJc9rqCAY4` (RHS "Grow at Home") cited in Sources. Requires: confirm the video is still live, add button + attribution link.

### 3. services/tree pages — coastal/Broadstairs-derived content

Residual content from the Broadstairs scaffold remains on three pages:

- `services/tree/index.html` (line 71): "coastal hedge specialist"
- `services/tree/pruning.html` (line 34): "Bridge seafront plots", "Stone Road", "salt-wind"
- `services/tree/crown-reduction.html` (line 48): "coastal Monterey pine" in species list

Bridge is an inland Nailbourne-valley/North Downs village. All three references are off-topic and should be rewritten. Requires a judgment call on replacement content.

### 4. highways-act-section-154-overhanging-vegetation-notice.html — @graph JSON-LD violation

The JSON-LD block wraps the `Article` in an `@graph` array alongside `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage` types. Per the template contract, individual how-to articles must use a flat `Article` (no `@graph`). The `@graph` pattern is reserved for `how-to/index.html` (CollectionPage) and the home page only. Requires rewriting the LD block to remove the `@graph` wrapper, keeping `Article` flat and dropping `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage`. Judgment call because FAQPage carries real FAQ content.

### 5. highways-act-section-154-overhanging-vegetation-notice.html — no video companion

The article has no "Watch first" callout and no `yt-play` button. It covers a legal/procedural topic (Highways Act s.154 notice) where a natural video companion may be harder to source than for species-care articles. Requires identifying a suitable real UK video or making a decision whether this article should be exempt from the video-companion requirement.

### 6. og:type missing from 16 content pages

The following pages carry no `og:type` meta property:

- `jobs/index.html`
- `services/index.html`, `services/hedge-cutting.html`, `services/hedge-reduction.html`
- `services/tree/index.html`, `services/tree/pruning.html`, `services/tree/crown-thinning.html`, `services/tree/crown-raising.html`, `services/tree/crown-reduction.html`, `services/tree/deadwooding.html`
- `about.html`, `404.html`, `contact.html` (not listed in previous reports — needs checking), `privacy.html`, `areas/index.html`, `thanks.html`, `how-to/index.html`

Note: `og:type` value varies by page type (`website` for the home page, `article` for articles, likely `website` for service/info pages). Requires a decision on the correct value per page type and a systematic add across these pages.

### 7. YouTube video health — unverifiable

The session proxy environment blocks YouTube HEAD requests, so the HTTP status of all 33 video watch URLs cannot be confirmed. No iframe 404 pages have been reported by any other mechanism. This check remains pending until the proxy restrictions change or verification is done outside this environment.
