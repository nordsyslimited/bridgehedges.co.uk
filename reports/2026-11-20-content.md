## Bridge / Nailbourne-valley specificity

The article addresses:

1. **Chalk soil compatibility** - osmanthus burkwoodii is fully tolerant of the alkaline chalk soils of the North Downs above Bridge (pH 7.5+), contrasted explicitly with acid-loving plants (camellia, rhododendron, mophead hydrangeas) that fail on this ground.
2. **Nesting-season timing conflict** - post-flowering pruning (late April / May) falls squarely in the nesting season. s.1 Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 cited. Visual inspection step described.
3. **Nailbourne valley frost pocket** - the April flowers (not the plant itself) can be caught by late frosts in the frost-pocket valley-floor gardens of Bishopsbourne and Barham. The plant is H5 fully hardy; the risk is to the annual flowering display only.
4. **Bifrons Park Conservation Area** - osmanthus grows slowly and rarely reaches the 75mm stem-diameter s.211 threshold, but the check is noted for old specimens. Link to the CA notice guide provided.
5. **Box substitute** - osmanthus as a chalk-tolerant evergreen alternative to box for 1-2m hedges in a post-blight / post-box-tree-moth context.
